01 / Product Overview

Evidence-Led Barrier Management

Access Haryana Campus demonstrates an auditable, end-to-end workflow for managing campus accessibility improvements. The platform enables auditors, facilities teams, and accessibility verifiers to collaborate on identifying, prioritizing, and resolving accessibility barriers through a structured, traceable process.

02 / User Journey

Six Screens, One Workflow

The application guides users through a complete accessibility management cycle. Each screen represents a distinct role perspective and decision point in the barrier-to-fix workflow.

Screen 1: Overview (Command Centre)

Entry point displaying the current active journey, next actions, backlog of barriers, campus evidence, the evidence lifecycle rail, activity timeline, and role switcher.

Screen 2: Guided Audit

Progressive discovery of accessibility barriers with structured questionnaire, evidence capture, barrier description, and immediate impact assessment.

Screen 3: Barrier Record

Detailed barrier documentation with measurements, evidence images, standards references, and priority assignment for facilities team review.

Screen 4: Work Order Management

Facilities team interface for repair coordination including scope, resource allocation, timeline, and completion tracking.

Screen 5: Verification

Independent retest interface where verifiers confirm repairs meet audit criteria and record journey success rates.

Screen 6: Impact Report

Executive dashboard with verified repair count, journey success metrics, cost analysis, and exportable summary.
